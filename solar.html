Solar characterisation rig — detailed wiring

2–3 panels in parallel · CN3791 · one 18650 · ESP32-S3-DevKitC (battery powered, deep-sleeps) · IP67 box · logs to Home Assistant
Golden rules: wire everything dead (no cell in the holder, panels covered or face-down) · panels connect last · every ground ends up on the same breadboard rail · nothing above 3.3 V ever touches a GPIO directly.

Three ways to let the smoke out, avoided: 6 V panel voltage straight into a GPIO (always through Divider A) · USB and battery-rail-to-5V at the same time (pull the 5V wire when flashing) · CHRG pad without its 10k (node sits ~4 V when idle).
Deep-sleep gotcha, so it doesn't surprise you: the ESP is only awake 25 s in every 5 min. To reflash later, either catch it awake (watch HA for the entities updating) or flip the prevent deep sleep switch ESPHome creates in HA, then flash on the next wake.
Recording: three HA entities — panel V, battery V, Solar Charging on/off. History gives charging-hours/day and the battery curve; that's the panel verdict. Config: solar-test/solar-logger.yaml (INA219 block ready, commented out).
