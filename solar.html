Solar characterisation rig

How much can the little panel really pump out, charted per-day · ESP32-S3-DevKitC + breadboard · IP67 box · logs to Home Assistant
The question this answers: real harvested Wh/day from the 6 V 150 mA panel through the CN3791, across real weather — vs the unit's consumption. That one chart decides panel count and battery size for the fence units.

Wiring — hardware already on the bench

Two rules that keep the smoke in: the ESP32's pins are 3.3 V — panel and battery voltages go through the dividers, never direct. And the shunt goes in the negative leg (low-side), so GPIO2 only ever sees millivolts.
Recording: the rig appears in Home Assistant as four entities — panel V, panel mA, harvest mW, and "Solar Harvest Today (Wh)" which resets at midnight. HA's history keeps every day automatically; a bar-chart card of daily harvest vs the unit's daily consumption is the whole deliverable. Config: solar-test/solar-logger.yaml in the repo.
Upgrade path (optional, ~$6): an INA219 current sensor replaces the shunt + both dividers with one I²C part and lab-grade accuracy. ESPHome supports it natively — four wires, five lines of YAML. Worth adding to the next parts order; not needed to start.
