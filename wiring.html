1 · The tester — today's job (modem dial-out)

Three signal wires, that's all. TXD0→RXD and RXD0→TXD (they cross — transmit into receive), GND↔GND always connected. No permanent power wire between the two boards: the red wire is only the 2-second wake touch from 3V3 to PWR. The firmware does everything else on its own — you just wire, power, wake, and watch the map.
If silence after wake: the loopback test said the fault is on the RAK pins — before re-checking the Waveshare end, confirm you're on the pins the 13002 silkscreen calls TXD0 / RXD0 (not TXD1/RXD1, not the SPI row).

2 · The full kit — fence MVP unit

Power: one protected 18650 feeds both boards (both take a 3.7 V Li-ion input); the solar panel keeps it topped up through the CN3791. In the field nothing else charges it — don't have both boards' USB leads plugged in at once while the battery is bridged, that's two chargers arguing. Sensors: four shared wires plus one XSHUT each — the firmware wakes them one at a time to give each its own I2C address, which is why every sensor needs its private IO line. Radios: LoRa antenna and LTE antenna both fitted before power-up, mounted clear of the fence's metal.
Build order that can't hurt anything: wire everything with the PSU/battery disconnected → double-check the six XSHUT lines against silkscreen → connect battery → meter sanity check → then USB only if you want the transcript.
