Six-slot sensor wiring — TOF050C on unit 3

RAK19001 + RAK4631 + RAK13002 · VL6180X sensors · shared I²C, one enable line each
Power pin is labelled VCC on the RAK13002 — there is no pin marked 3V3 on that header. VCC is the base board's regulated 3.3 V rail; that's the one. (The pair of GND pins beside it are the ground bus.)
Golden rule, still: read the silkscreen, never count pin positions. Wire everything with power disconnected, check it twice, then power up.

Why each sensor needs its own enable wire

Every VL6180X leaves the factory with the same I²C address (0x29). Six of them on one bus would all answer at once and the bus would be useless. The fix is the pin your boards label SHUT (aka CE / XSHUT — same pin, different vendors' names): the firmware holds all six sensors in reset, then wakes them one at a time, giving each a new address as it comes up — 0x30, 0x31, 0x32 and so on.

That is why the four bus wires are shared by everyone, and the fifth wire is private to each sensor. Get a SHUT wire on the wrong pin and that slot reports the wrong container.

Slots 5 and 6 — the NFC trap. P0.09 and P0.10 are the nRF52840's NFC antenna pins and are not GPIO by default. Wire them perfectly and both slots will still read as faulted. Fixed in firmware by -DCONFIG_NFCT_PINS_AS_GPIOS, which I have now added to firmware/unit/platformio.ini. It writes the chip's UICR on first boot and needs one power cycle to take effect. If exactly slots 5 and 6 fail, this is why — not the sensors.
Slot 2 goes to SW1, not IO2 — this is deliberate. The RAK13002 has no IO2 pad at all; it breaks out IO1, IO3, IO4, IO5, IO6, IO7 (confirmed in RAK's datasheet). The firmware originally asked for WB_IO2, which does not exist on this module, so slot 2 could never have worked. It now uses WB_SW1 — the free GPIO on that header, printed on the silkscreen as SW1. A0 is battery sense and A1 is the charger status line, so both are off limits.

The shared bus — four wires, all six sensors

Each sensor, one at a time

Every TOF050C gets five wires: four shared, one private. Wire them in slot order and label each CE wire as you go — a mislabelled SHUT wire is invisible until the data is wrong.

Slot 1 — SHUT to IO1

VIN → VCCGND → GNDSDA → SDA1SCL → SCL1SHUT → IO1

Slot 2 — SHUT to SW1

VIN → VCCGND → GNDSDA → SDA1SCL → SCL1SHUT → SW1
Yes, SW1 — the RAK13002 has no IO2 pin. The firmware is set to match.

Power up on the bench supply with the limit at ~100 mA and watch the current before anything else. Six sensors idle at a few mA; a jump to the limit means a short — kill it and recheck.

What happens when you power on

The firmware holds every CE low (all sensors asleep), then brings them up one at a time and readdresses each. On the console you get a boot line listing which sensors answered:

{"boot":...,"sensors_ok":[1,1,1,1,1,1]}

ResultMeans
All six 1Wiring is right. Send RANGE to see live millimetres per slot
Exactly slots 5 and 6 zeroThe NFC pin flag — reflash with the fix, power cycle once
One slot zeroThat sensor's CE wire, or its power/ground
All six zeroThe shared bus — SDA/SCL swapped, or no power to the sensors

Occupied/empty flips at an 80 mm threshold. RANGE reports raw millimetres per slot and is the tool for setting that threshold against real cradle geometry.
