How the CN3791 works, in one breath: it sits between panel and cell only. When the sun's up it pulls the panel at its best-power voltage (that's the MPPT part) and pushes the energy into the cell, tapering off and stopping at 4.2 V. When there's no sun it does nothing and drains almost nothing. The RAK and modem never talk to it — they just drink from the cell, day and night. Red LED = charging, green = full, both dark at night = normal.
Bench cautions: no USB leads in either board while the shared cell is connected (two on-board chargers would fight over one cell). Double-check holder polarity before the cell goes in — reverse polarity kills the CN3791 instantly. And the modem still needs its 2-second 3V3→PWR wake tap after power-up.

2 · Sensors — the 4 wired slots

Each sensor: the same four bus wires (VIN, GND, SDA, SCL — daisy-chained), plus one private SHUT wire. The sensor pin is silkscreened SHUT; INT stays empty. Boot check: sensors_ok [1,1,1,1,0,0] is the expected happy state tonight — the two 0s are the unwired slots 5/6, and the firmware carries them as faults without complaint (nothing downspecced — wire them later, they light up).
