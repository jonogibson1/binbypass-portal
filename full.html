Bench-only power caution: don't have both boards' USB leads plugged in while the single cell is bridged across both — that's two chargers fighting. On the bench, power from the cell (or the PSU-as-battery rig at 4.00 V) and use USB for data only.
The two hard parts, spelled out:
• Slot 2 → SW1, not IO2. The RAK13002 has no IO2 pad; SW1 is the free GPIO. Already set in firmware.
• Slots 5 & 6 → IO5/IO6, which are the nRF52840's NFC pins. They are not GPIO until CONFIG_NFCT_PINS_AS_GPIOS writes the chip's UICR — which only takes effect after a power cycle, and sticks across reflashes. If those two read as faults, that's the first suspect, not the sensors or your wiring.

Antennas (for the enclosure, not the breadboard)

Four MHF1/u.FL leads off the carrier into the plastic cap: LTE FXUB63 (97 mm), LoRa FXP890 (62 mm), GNSS AGGP.25F (25 mm patch, flat under the ABS), BLE FXP73 (47 mm). Full detail in the eng repo's ANTENNA-BOM.md.
